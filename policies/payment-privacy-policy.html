1. Introduction

This Payment Privacy Policy explains how BADSHAH AI (Badshah222 AI), created by Badshah (Zain Shah) in Swabi, Pakistan, handles your payment information. Payments on BADSHAH AI are manual: there is no automatic online checkout and no card payment form. You pay first using one of the methods below, and then submit an order form with your payer name and a payment screenshot so the admin can verify your payment and activate your Pro plan.

3. What Payment Information We Collect

When you submit a Pro order, we collect only two things:

  • Payer name: the name of the person who sent the payment.
  • Payment screenshot: a screenshot or photo of your payment confirmation, as proof that you paid.

We collect these for one purpose only: to verify your Pro order and match the payment to your account. We do not collect your bank account password, mobile wallet PIN, OTP codes, card numbers, or any other banking credentials. We will never ask you for them — if anyone claiming to be from BADSHAH AI asks for your password, PIN, or OTP, do not share it and report it to us immediately through the Help Center.

4. How Your Payment Information Is Stored

Your payer name and payment screenshot are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. They are visible only to the admin of BADSHAH AI. No other user, and no third party, can see them. They are protected by the same access controls that protect the rest of our Supabase backend.

5. How Your Payment Information Is Used — and Not Used

Your payment information is used only to:

  • Verify that your Pro payment was received;
  • Match the payment to your account and Pro plan order;
  • Activate your Pro plan after approval, or contact you if there is a problem with your order.

It is never shared, sold, rented, or given to any third party. It is never used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose other than processing your Pro order. We never send your payment details to the AI API provider or to any payment processor, because payments are manual and handled directly between you and us.

6. How Long We Keep Payment Records

Payment screenshots and order records are kept while your order is being processed, and afterwards for record-keeping — so we can confirm your plan, resolve any later dispute about a payment, and keep proper accounts. If you ask for your account to be deleted, your payment records may be kept in anonymised or summary form where needed for accounting and fraud prevention.

7. Refunds on Manual Payments

Because payments are manual, refunds are handled case by case by the admin. If you paid twice by mistake, paid the wrong amount, or your order was rejected (for example, because the payment could not be verified), contact us through the in-app Help Center with your order details and payment proof. The admin will review the case and decide on a refund or adjustment. Once a Pro plan has been activated and used, the payment is generally non-refundable — see the Terms & Conditions for full details.

8. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for sending your payment to the correct account shown in Section 2, and for the correct amount for the plan you chose. BADSHAH AI is not responsible for money sent to a wrong number or account, or to a person pretending to be us. Always confirm the payment details on this page or in the app before paying, and keep your payment receipt until your Pro plan is activated.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Payment Privacy Policy if our payment methods or process change. Any update will change the "Last updated" date above, and, where reasonable, we will notify users in the app.

10. Contact Us

For any question about a payment, an order, or a refund, contact us through the in-app Help Center, or on WhatsApp at 03251787969. Please have your payer name, payment method, and payment screenshot ready so we can help you quickly.
