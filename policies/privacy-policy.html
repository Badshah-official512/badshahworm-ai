Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

BADSHAH AI (Badshah222 AI) is a ChatGPT-style AI web application created by Badshah (Zain Shah), based in Swabi, Pakistan. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use BADSHAH AI, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information: your name and email address, provided when you register or log in.
  • Password: your password is handled by Supabase Auth and stored only in hashed form. It is never stored in plain text, and our team can never see your password.
  • Chats and prompts: your conversations with the AI are stored locally on your own device in IndexedDB and localStorage, so you can continue your chats and view your history. They are not uploaded to our servers by default.
  • Usage and token counts: we record how many requests and tokens you use, so we can enforce Free and Pro plan limits and keep the service fair for everyone.
  • Login security information: your login IP address and device information are logged for security purposes, to help detect unauthorised access to your account.
  • Payment information: when you order a Pro plan, we collect your payer name and a payment screenshot (proof of payment). We never ask for, and never collect, your bank password, PIN, or OTP codes.
  • Help messages: messages you send through the in-app Help Center, so we can answer your questions and resolve problems.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service — to run your AI chats and image generation, and to keep you logged in.
  • To verify Pro orders — to match your payment with your account and activate your Pro plan after the admin approves it.
  • For security — to protect accounts from unauthorised access and to prevent abuse of the service.
  • For support — to reply to your Help Center messages and fix problems you report.
  • To improve the service — to understand usage patterns, for example which models are popular, so we can improve the app.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

4. Supabase — Our Data Processor and Host

BADSHAH AI is built on Supabase. Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and file storage, and acts as our data processor. Your account details, plan status, orders, and uploaded payment screenshots are stored in Supabase. Your password is handled entirely by Supabase Auth in hashed form. Supabase stores data under its own security and privacy standards, and we rely on those safeguards to protect your information.

5. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. Your information is shared only in these limited situations:

  • Supabase: our hosting and database provider, which stores your account, plan, and order data as described above.
  • The AI API provider: when you send a message or generate an image, your prompt is sent to our upstream AI API provider so it can generate the answer. Only the prompt itself is sent — not your name, email, or payment details.
  • Legal requirements: if required by the law of Pakistan or by a valid legal order, we may disclose information as legally required.

Your payment screenshots are visible only to the admin of BADSHAH AI, and to no one else.

6. Your Rights and Controls

  • Your chats: you can view, export, and delete your chats inside the app at any time, because they are stored on your own device.
  • Your account: you can request correction of your account details, or request deletion of your account and its associated data, by contacting us through the in-app Help Center.
  • Pro plan: you can check your current plan and its expiry date in the app.

When you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your account data from our database, except for payment records we must keep for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account and plan data for as long as your account is active. Payment screenshots and order records are kept while your order is processed and afterwards for record-keeping. Login security logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted. Chats stored on your device remain there until you delete them yourself.

8. Children's Use

BADSHAH AI is intended for users aged 13 and above. If you are under 13, you may only use the service with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a feature or change a provider. If we make a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where reasonable, notify users in the app. Continued use of BADSHAH AI after a change means you accept the updated policy.
